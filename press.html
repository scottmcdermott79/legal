Word Drop

Can you unscramble the words in time to save the princess?

Word Drop turns the classic word puzzle into a race against the clock. Help Sir Voweliant rescue the princess: unscramble the falling letters to spell words, and each correct answer raises the bridge higher. Hesitate and the bridge drops — run out of time and the rescue fails. It's the calm of a word game with the pulse of an arcade challenge.

When you're stuck, four helpers keep the momentum going — Synonyms, Extra Time, New Word, and Reveal Letter — so a tough word never ends the run. Four difficulty levels, from Easy all the way to Impossible, keep it approachable for newcomers and punishing for word-game veterans. Every level has its own original music, and players can unlock badges and share their achievements with friends.

Word Bridge

Can you find the hidden words to help Bridget cross the river?

In Word Bridge, every puzzle hides a word snaking through a grid of letters, forming a path from one side of the screen to the other. Find it, trace it, and you've built Bridget a bridge across the river. Each game strings five grids together, and the goal is simple: clear all five as fast as you can. Grid sizes scale with difficulty, so the challenge grows as your eye sharpens. It's the second title from Water Mill Games and shares the studio's signature blend of wordplay and against-the-clock energy.
