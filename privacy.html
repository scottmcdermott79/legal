Water Mill Games LLC ("Water Mill Games," "we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile game Drop Word (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App. By downloading or playing Drop Word, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

👤 Important Notice for Parents and Guardians

Drop Word is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We are committed to protecting children's privacy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users. Please read this policy carefully.

1. Information We Collect

Drop Word is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information from you directly. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • We do not collect payment information. All in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • We do not use advertising networks or sell your data to third parties.

2. Automatically Collected Information (Apple Analytics)

If you have opted in to share analytics with app developers through your device settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous information about how the App is used, including:

  • App launch frequency and session duration.
  • Features used and general gameplay patterns.
  • Crash reports and performance data.

This information is aggregated and anonymized — it cannot be used to identify you personally.

You can control this sharing in your iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Apple's Privacy Policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

3. In-App Purchases

Drop Word may offer optional in-app purchases. All transactions are processed directly by Apple through the App Store. Water Mill Games LLC:

  • Never receives, stores, or processes your payment card information.
  • Does not have access to your Apple ID password or billing details.

All purchases are subject to Apple's Terms: apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Drop Word is rated 4+ and we take children's privacy seriously. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store guidelines for apps rated 4+:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
  • We do not display behavioral advertising.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not include social features, chat, or user-generated content.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete such information.

5. Third-Party Services

Beyond Apple's platform services described above, Drop Word does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or social platforms. Your gameplay remains entirely on your device.

6. Data Security

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data stored on our servers. Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device and may be backed up through iCloud if you have iCloud backup enabled — this is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
